Operational Requirements

v1.0 Specifications
Code Requirement Tolerance Associated Feature
1.B.1 Emergency Stop: Car halts if an obstacle is within 10 cm in front. Stop distance ≤ 10 cm F3, F4 (Safety & Nav)
2.A.1 Instantaneous Power Measurement: Measure and display current/voltage per module. 5% error, 1 Hz refresh F1 (Energy)
2.A.2 Cumulative Energy Tracking: Calculate total energy consumed since startup (Wh). 5% error (Wh) F2 (Energy)
3.A.1 SLAM Map Localization: Estimate car position on generated map. Max error = 10 cm F3 (Navigation)
4.A.1 Waste AI Recognition: Detect cans/bottles on ground up to 1.5 m via camera. > 80% detection rate F6, F9 (AI Vision)
6.A.1 Robotic Arm Pick & Place: Retrieve identified waste and place in container. > 70% success / 10 trials F5 (Robotic Arm)
