Schedule Control & Agile Methodology

Frequent Activity Reports

Bi-weekly reports structured around three key sync questions: what was completed, difficulties encountered, and upcoming tasks. Rotating Scrum Master and Product Owner roles each sprint foster complete team agility.

Velocity & Capacity (91 Points/Week)

Task complexity is estimated using a story-point system matching work hours. For our 7-member team at 13h/week/person, maximum weekly capacity is capped at 91 points to maintain schedule discipline.
